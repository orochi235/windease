# Edge scroll

Dragging a pane toward the edge of a scrolling container scrolls it, and keeps
scrolling while the cursor is held there.

<DragToTheEdgeToScroll />

**Nothing to turn on.** A container with a `scrollRef`
([4.8](/?story=guide--04-layout--4-08-telling-windease-where-the-scroll-got-to))
auto-scrolls; one without never does.

The rate ramps from zero at 48px out to 16px per frame at the edge, and **holds
there past it** so overshooting a target does not fight you.

**React**

```tsx
<Container scrollRef={scrollRef} edgeScroll={{ margin: 64, maxRate: 24 }} />
```

The two numbers above are the defaults. The presets take no `scrollRef`, so
neither auto-scroll nor `edgeScroll` reaches them.

**Core**

```ts
const delta = edgeScrollDelta(bounds, point, { margin: 64, maxRate: 24 });
```

Pure and exported, for a host driving its own drag loop. `DropTargetOptions`
takes the same bag.

## It is a tuning bag, not a policy

`edgeScroll` reshapes a ramp. There is nothing to refuse and nothing to defer
to, which is exactly what separates it from the three replaceable policies in
[§8](/?story=guide--08-customization--8-04-the-policy-contract) — a distinction
worth keeping straight, because it takes a similar-looking prop.
